Exit Timing Calendar

Turn a private-markets tracker into a year-by-year picture of when cash might actually arrive. It will not give you a predicted exit date, because nobody has one.

Drop your tracker here

An .xlsx or .csv with one row per position. Or pick a file.

🔒 Everything runs in your browser. Your file is never uploaded anywhere.
Not advice. The outcome probabilities are assumptions and are meant to be argued with. Source and the tracker template are in the repository.
